# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free collaboration environment.
Contributors must communicate constructively, respect privacy and attribution,
and avoid discriminatory, threatening, or disruptive behavior.

Repository maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and may temporarily
or permanently restrict participation when conduct harms the community or the
project. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's security
reporting channel; do not publish personal or sensitive details in an issue.
